<h2>RimWorld Traits FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Can colonists have more than 2 traits?</h3>
  <p>Normally 1–2. Some backgrounds, genes (Biotech) or MODs add traits. Starting pawns roll random traits.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Which traits pair well together?</h3>
  <p>Sanguine + Industrious is a strong all-rounder. Tough + Brawler makes a great frontline fighter.</p>
