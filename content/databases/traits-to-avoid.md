<p>Some <strong>RimWorld traits</strong> actively hurt your colony. Here's the list of <strong>traits to avoid</strong>, especially on starting colonists, for the current <strong>1.6 / Odyssey</strong> version.</p>

<h2>Traits to Avoid on Starting Colonists</h2>
<table>
<thead><tr><th>Trait</th><th>Why to avoid</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Pyromaniac</td><td>Randomly starts fires — threatens base and pawns.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Chemical Interest</td><td>Random drug binges and addiction risk.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Lazy</td><td>Global work speed -20%, drags productivity.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Wimp</td><td>Downed easily by pain — a liability in combat.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Greedy / Jealous</td><td>Require impressive bedrooms or they get unhappy.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Abrasive</td><td>Frequent social fights, lowers colony mood.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Too Slow</td><td>Move speed -20%, slower hauling and defense.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<h2>How to Handle Bad Traits</h2>
<p>If a good pawn has a bad trait, you can: give them a better bedroom (Greedy/Jealous), keep them away from the stockpile (Pyromaniac), or assign them non-combat work (Wimp). For starts, simply re-roll.</p>

<h2>RimWorld Traits FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Can bad traits be removed?</h3>
  <p>Some MODs and Biotech genes can modify traits, but vanilla pawns keep their traits. Manage around them or pick different colonists.</p>
