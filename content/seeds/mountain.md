<h2>Why a Mountain Base Wins</h2>
<p>Mountain bases need fewer walls, absorb raids at choke points, and protect against orbital events. The tradeoff is slower expansion and mining work.</p>

<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Are mountain maps safer?</h3>
  <p>Yes for raids — natural rock blocks most approaches. But mining takes time and infestations can spawn in deep mountains.</p>
