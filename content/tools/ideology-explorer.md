<h2>Designing Your Ideoligion</h2>
<p>Precepts shape your colony's mood rules. Pick a <strong>consistent theme</strong>: a warrior cult pairs raiding-approved with voluntary pain; a high-life colony pairs intense drugs with luxury. Rituals on short cooldowns (drum parties) give steady mood without big costs.</p>

<h2>RimWorld Ideology FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Do precepts affect pawn happiness?</h3>
  <p>Yes — matching precepts give mood bonuses (e.g., eating meat when meat is venerated), while violating them causes penalties.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Which ritual is best early game?</h3>
  <p>Drum parties are cheap and cooldown-friendly; sacrifice and blinding pay more but need resources or a willing pawn.</p>
</div>
