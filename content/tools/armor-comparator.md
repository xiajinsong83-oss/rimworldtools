<h2>Why Armor Type Matters</h2>
<p>RimWorld damage splits into <strong>sharp</strong> (bullets, swords), <strong>blunt</strong> (maces, rams) and <strong>heat</strong> (fire, plasma). Armor protects differently against each — marine armor stops sharp best, while flak covers blunt well.</p>

<h2>RimWorld Armor FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>What's the best armor in the game?</h3>
  <p>Cataphract and prestige marine armor give the highest protection. For early game, flak vest + helmet is the best value.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Does armor block everything?</h3>
  <p>No — it reduces damage by a percentage. High armor penetration weapons (charge, rockets) pierce more.</p>
