<h2>Choosing the Right Weapon</h2>
<p>Against <strong>unarmored raiders</strong>, snipers and charge rifles excel. Against <strong>mechanoids</strong> (heavy armor), bring EMP grenades and blunt weapons, or target the charge rifle's piercing shots. Quality beats base damage — a masterwork pistol out-damages a normal rifle.</p>
