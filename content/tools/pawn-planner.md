<h2>What Makes a Good Starting Colonist?</h2>
<p>A strong colony start needs a balanced crew: one dedicated <strong>cook/doctor</strong>, one <strong>builder/miner</strong>, and one <strong>researcher/grower</strong>. Look for traits that boost work speed (<strong>Industrious</strong>) or mood stability (<strong>Sanguine</strong>).</p>

<h2>RimWorld Pawn FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>How do skill levels affect pawns?</h3>
  <p>Higher skill means faster and higher-quality work. Skills 1–20 plus passions (minor/major) boost speed and quality further.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Should I min-max starting colonists?</h3>
  <p>Balance is safer than min-maxing early. Avoid pawns with negative traits like <strong>Pyromaniac</strong> or <strong>Chemical Interest</strong> at colony start.</p>
