<h2>What Affects Impressiveness</h2>
<p>Four factors matter: <strong>wealth</strong> (silver value), <strong>beauty</strong> (decorations/quality), <strong>space</strong> (walkable tiles), and <strong>cleanliness</strong>. The formula averages all four, then pulls the score down toward the <em>weakest</em> factor — so a dirty room hurts even a rich one.</p>

<h2>RimWorld Room Impressiveness FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>How do I reach "Wondrously Impressive" (240+)?</h3>
  <p>You need high values in all four factors. Best value: high-quality sculptures (beauty + wealth) plus a clean, spacious room.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Does cleanliness matter more than beauty?</h3>
  <p>Cleanliness is a full factor. Keeping a room clean is usually the cheapest way to lift impressiveness from Dull to Normal+.</p>
