<h2>Feeding a Colony: What to Plant</h2>
<p>Early game: <strong>rice</strong> fills the freezer fast. Mid game: <strong>corn</strong> gives the best food per tile on rich soil, <strong>potatoes</strong> thrive on poor soil, and <strong>haygrass</strong> feeds animals. Add <strong>cotton</strong> for clothing and <strong>healroot</strong> for medicine.</p>

<h2>RimWorld Crop FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Which crop gives the most food per tile?</h3>
  <p>Corn yields the most per tile over a season on fertile soil; rice is fastest but feeds less per tile.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>How many tiles of crops does a colonist need?</h3>
  <p>Roughly 8-12 tiles of rice or corn per colonist, plus buffer for winter and blights.</p>
