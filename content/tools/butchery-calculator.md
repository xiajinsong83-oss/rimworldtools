<h2>Butchering Tips</h2>
<p>Use a <strong>butcher table</strong> (not a spot) for more yield, and keep the butchering bill on a dedicated pawn. <strong>Cows</strong> give the best meat-to-feed ratio; <strong>muffalo</strong> add leather and wool; thrumbos are a jackpot if you can take one down.</p>

<h2>RimWorld Butchery FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Does the butcher table matter?</h3>
  <p>Yes — a proper butcher table yields more meat than a butcher spot, and a sterile one yields the most.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Should I butcher animals or sell them alive?</h3>
  <p>Live animals sell for more, but butchering gives meat and leather for your colony — balance based on your food stock.</p>
