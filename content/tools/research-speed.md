<h2>How to Research Faster</h2>
<p>Stack <strong>bench bonuses</strong> (advanced bench + multi-analyzer), assign a <strong>Too Smart / Fast Learner</strong> pawn, and keep the lab lit and clean. Each researcher doubles throughput, so two pawns finish roughly twice as fast.</p>
