<h2>Why Cooler Count Matters</h2>
<p>Too few coolers means food rots in summer; too many wastes power. <strong>Wall insulation, double doors and a small airlock</strong> let each cooler do more work, so you need fewer.</p>

<h2>RimWorld Freezer FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>How many coolers for a 200-tile freezer?</h3>
  <p>At 0°C in a temperate biome, roughly 2 coolers with a safety spare. Hot biomes need more.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Should I set coolers to -10°C?</h3>
  <p>Only for meat-heavy stockpiles. 0°C preserves most food; -10°C needs more coolers and power.</p>
