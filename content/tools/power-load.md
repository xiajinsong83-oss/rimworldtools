<h2>How to Avoid Brownouts</h2>
<p>Keep <strong>generation above consumption</strong> by a comfortable margin. Solar and wind fluctuate, so a battery bank and a spare generator prevent sudden brownouts during weather or raids.</p>

<h2>RimWorld Power FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Why does my base lose power at night?</h3>
  <p>Solar panels stop at night. Add batteries or a wind/chemfuel backup to cover the gap.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>What's the most power-hungry thing?</h3>
