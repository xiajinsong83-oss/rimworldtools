<h2>Best Traits for Starting Colonists</h2>
<p>For a strong colony start, favor <strong>Industrious</strong>, <strong>Sanguine</strong> and <strong>Too Smart</strong>. Avoid <strong>Pyromaniac</strong>, <strong>Chemical Interest</strong> and <strong>Lazy</strong> early on — they hurt colony stability.</p>

<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>How many traits can a colonist have?</h3>
