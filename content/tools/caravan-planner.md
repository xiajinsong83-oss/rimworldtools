<h2>Caravan Tips</h2>
<p>Bring a <strong>pack animal per 100 kg</strong> of cargo and <strong>survival meals</strong> for the trip. Travel light: heavy caravans crawl and get raided more. Horses and muffalo are the best all-round pack animals.</p>

  <h3>How much can a colonist carry?</h3>
  <p>About 75 kg baseline; pack animals add 60–250 kg each. Donkeys are cheap early-game carriers.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
