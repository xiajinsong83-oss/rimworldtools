<h2>What to Raise</h2>
<p><strong>Chickens</strong> are cheap and give eggs; <strong>cows</strong> give milk for fine meals; <strong>muffalo</strong> double as pack animals. Avoid huge animals (elephants) until you have a strong food surplus.</p>

<h2>RimWorld Livestock FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>How much hay does an animal eat per day?</h3>
  <p>Most animals eat 1-2 nutrition/day of hay; large ones (muffalo, elephant) eat 6-12. Plan fields for winter.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Can animals graze instead of eating hay?</h3>
  <p>Yes — animals eat grass when allowed to roam. Keep them inside or penned with a hay stockpile for winter.</p>
