<h2>Feeding the Colony</h2>
<p>Keep a <strong>10-day buffer</strong> of meals minimum; more before winter and sieges. <strong>Fine meals</strong> give a mood boost for little extra cost — upgrade from simple meals as soon as you have a cook and ingredients. <strong>Nutrient paste</strong> is a great emergency option (no food poisoning).</p>
