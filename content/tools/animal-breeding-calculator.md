<h2>Breeding Strategy</h2>
<p><strong>Chickens and rabbits</strong> explode in numbers quickly (watch feed!); <strong>pigs</strong> are the fast meat farm; <strong>cows</strong> breed slowly but pay off with milk and beef. Keep one male per several females for steady output.</p>
