<h2>Climate Strategy</h2>
<p><strong>Cold biomes</strong> (tundra, ice sheet): build heaters early, keep rooms compact and insulated with double walls. <strong>Hot biomes</strong> (desert, tropical): coolers are essential for food storage and bedrooms. A <strong>freezer at -10°C</strong> is non-negotiable in every biome once you have a food surplus.</p>

<h2>RimWorld Temperature FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Why is my freezer not cold enough?</h3>
  <p>Check power, wall insulation (double walls), and roof coverage — uncovered rooms lose temperature fast.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>How many heaters does a large base need?</h3>
  <p>Roughly one industrial heater per large room in cold biomes; compact rooms need fewer.</p>
</div>
