<h2>How Raids Scale</h2>
<p>Raid size scales mostly with <strong>colony wealth</strong> and colonist count. Too much unused item wealth (clothes, resources, loot) inflates raids beyond your defenses. Spend or consume it to keep raids manageable.</p>

<h2>RimWorld Raid FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Why are my raids so big?</h3>
  <p>High colony wealth is the main driver. Banked silver and stockpiled items all count toward threat.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>How do I lower raid threat?</h3>
  <p>Spend wealth on defenses and consumables, keep stockpiles lean, and avoid hoarding low-value items.</p>
