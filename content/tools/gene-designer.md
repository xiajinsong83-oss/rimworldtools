<h2>Designing a Good Xenotype</h2>
<p>Xenogenes cost <strong>metabolic points</strong>; negative-cost genes (weaknesses) pay for strong positives. A classic soldier xenotype pairs <strong>Tough + Fast Walker + Super Immunity</strong> offset by <strong>Nearsighted + Bloodlust</strong> or similar negatives. Endogenes are inherited — plan lineage carefully.</p>

<h2>RimWorld Gene FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>What's the difference between xenogenes and endogenes?</h3>
  <p>Xenogenes are installed in a gene assembler and don't pass to children; endogenes are inherited and can't be freely changed.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Can I give a colonist too many good genes?</h3>
  <p>Yes — positive genes push the metabolic cost over budget. Offset with negative genes or archite capsules.</p>
</div>
