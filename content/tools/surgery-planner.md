<h2>How to Make Surgery Safer</h2>
<p>Use a <strong>clean medical bed in a lit room</strong>, a <strong>high-Medical doctor</strong>, and anesthesia. Failed surgeries damage the patient — never operate with a low-skill doctor or a dirty bed.</p>

<h2>RimWorld Surgery FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>What happens if surgery fails?</h3>
  <p>Failed operations damage body parts or kill the patient on catastrophic failure. High skill and cleanliness prevent this.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Which surgeries are worth the risk?</h3>
  <p>Peg legs, bionic eyes and arms are low-risk, high-value. Heart and brain implants are riskier — wait for a good doctor.</p>
