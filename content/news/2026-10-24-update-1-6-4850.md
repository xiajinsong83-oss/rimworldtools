<p>The RimWorld 1.6.4850 update arrived mid-October with a small but colony-shaping changelog: explosion-immune heavy bridges, zero-wealth ancient bridges for watery maps, forbiddable embryos, and a long list of stability fixes across Odyssey content. Compatible with all savegames and mods.</p>

<h2>Wealth management: ancient bridges that cost nothing</h2>
<p>Landing on maps where ruins generate over water used to silently add a large amount of buildable structure to your wealth, pushing raid strength up before you had defenses. 1.6.4850 adds <strong>ancient bridges and ancient heavy bridges with a market value of 0</strong> to stop those wealth spikes — ruins over water are now effectively free structures. For wealth-conscious players this is a meaningful change when scouting map seeds and starting positions.</p>

<h2>Defense building: heavy bridges now shrug off explosions</h2>
<p><strong>Heavy bridges are immune to explosion damage</strong> in 1.6.4850, while a separate fix ensures regular bridges are correctly destroyed by explosions (they previously survived too often). Heavy bridges can now anchor defensive chokepoints over water and shallow terrain against raiders with frag grenades and rocket launchers — pair them with embrasures from the Defense Planner toolset for a budget killbox.</p>

<h2>Life management: embryos and other notable fixes</h2>
<p>Embryos are now <strong>forbiddable</strong>, letting you lock down growth vats and stop pawns hauling embryos you want to keep in place. The same patch fixes babies deleted when born during shuttle flight, EMP rounds failing to break low shields, gravship jumps interrupting deathrest, mechanoid repair rates at low tick speed, and pawns endlessly wanting a looks-change when ideology and genes clash. See the official changelog for the full list.</p>
