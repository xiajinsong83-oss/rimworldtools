<h2>When to Use What</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Charge rifle:</strong> the all-rounder — great damage, good range, high armor piercing.</li>
<li><strong>Sniper rifle:</strong> pick off raiders and mechanoid turrets from range.</li>
<li><strong>Heavy SMG:</strong> close-range shredder for kill boxes.</li>
<li><strong>Monosword / plasma sword:</strong> for your melee specialist — pair with shield belt.</li>
<li><strong>EMP:</strong> always carry one against mechanoids.</li>
</ul>

<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Is the charge rifle the best gun?</h3>
  <p>For general use, yes — best damage-to-accuracy balance in the game. Snipers beat it at extreme range.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
