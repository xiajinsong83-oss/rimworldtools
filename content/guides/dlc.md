<p>There are <strong>5 RimWorld DLCs</strong> (including <strong>Odyssey</strong>). If you're wondering which to buy first, this guide ranks them by value for different playstyles.</p>

<h2>All 5 RimWorld DLCs</h2>
<table>
<thead><tr><th>DLC</th><th>Adds</th><th>Best for</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Royalty</td><td>Empire faction, titles, psionics, mech clusters</td><td>Players who like structured goals and magic-like powers.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Ideology</td><td>Belief systems, rituals, social roles, dryads</td><td>Story and roleplay players who enjoy customizing colonies.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Biotech</td><td>Children, mechanoids, genetics, xenohumans</td><td>Long-term colony builders who love depth and family lines.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Anomaly</td><td>Horror entities, containment, alternate endings</td><td>Players who want danger, mystery and a darker tone.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Odyssey</td><td>Gravships, inter-planet travel, moving colonies, space stations</td><td>Explorers who want to leave the planet and travel.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<h2>Our Recommendation</h2>
<p><strong>Biotech</strong> offers the most depth and replay value for most players. If you love exploration, <strong>Odyssey</strong> adds a whole new endgame. For a lighter, cheaper first step, <strong>Royalty</strong> is a safe pick.</p>
