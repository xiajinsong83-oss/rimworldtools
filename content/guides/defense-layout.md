<p>A good <strong>RimWorld defense layout</strong> is the difference between losing a colony and losing nothing. Here's how to design defenses for the current <strong>1.6 / Odyssey</strong> version.</p>

<h2>Core Defense Principles</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Funnel the enemy:</strong> walls force raiders through one choke point (kill box).</li>
<li><strong>Cover beats numbers:</strong> sandbags, embrasures and walls give your shooters a 50%+ edge.</li>
<li><strong>Turrets absorb:</strong> autocannons and mini-turrets tank fire while pawns shoot from cover.</li>
<li><strong>Multiple layers:</strong> a perimeter wall plus interior bunkers saves the colony when one line falls.</li>
</ul>

</tbody>
</table>

<h2>Defense FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Do kill boxes still work in 1.6?</h3>
  <p>Yes — raiders still path through openings. Keep the entrance clear of doors so they don't break walls instead.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>How much power do turrets need?</h3>
  <p>Mini-turrets draw ~75W each, autocannons ~400W — budget your grid with a dedicated defense circuit.</p>
</div>
