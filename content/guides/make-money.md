<p>Wondering <strong>how to make money in RimWorld</strong>? Here are the most reliable income sources for the current <strong>1.6 / Odyssey</strong> version.</p>

<h2>Quick Answer</h2>
<p>Tailoring and crafting (clothes, weapons, art) are the best stable income. Grow cash crops like smokeleaf or cotton, then sell goods at a trade outpost or caravan.</p>

<h2>Best Income Sources</h2>
<table>
<thead><tr><th>Method</th><th>Income potential</th><th>Effort</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Tailoring (craft clothes)</td><td>High, steady</td><td>Low–medium</td></tr>
<tr><td>Art / sculptures</td><td>Very high</td><td>Medium (needs Artistic pawn)</td></tr>
<tr><td>Cash crops (smokeleaf, cotton)</td><td>High</td><td>Low</td></tr>
<tr><td>Drugs (yayo, flake)</td><td>Very high</td><td>Low (has risks)</td></tr>
<tr><td>Caravan trading</td><td>High</td><td>Medium–high</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<h2>Tips</h2>
<ul>
<li>Sell to outlanders and settlements — prices are better than at your own colony.</li>
<li>Keep stockpiles lean: excess wealth inflates raids (see our <a href="/tools/raid-planner/">raid planner</a>).</li>
<li>Invest profits in defenses and better gear, not hoarding.</li>
</ul>

<h2>RimWorld Economy FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Is selling drugs worth it?</h3>
  <p>Yes — high profit, but keep your own colonists away from them to avoid addiction.</p>
</div>
