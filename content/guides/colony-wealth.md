<p><strong>RimWorld colony wealth</strong> is the single biggest driver of raid size. Learn what counts, how it scales, and how to spend it — for the current <strong>1.6 / Odyssey</strong> version.</p>

<h2>What Counts as Wealth</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Buildings and items:</strong> everything constructed and every item stored.</li>
<li><strong>Colonists and animals:</strong> pawns add significant wealth.</li>
<li><strong>Silver and valuables:</strong> raw silver, gold, components count fully.</li>
</ul>

<h2>How Wealth Scales Raids</h2>
<table>
<thead><tr><th>Wealth tier</th><th>Raid trend</th><th>Advice</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Under 50k</td><td>Light raids (1-3 raiders)</td><td>Focus on food and weapons</td></tr>
<tr><td>50k-200k</td><td>Moderate (4-8)</td><td>Build walls and turrets</td></tr>
<tr><td>200k-500k</td><td>Heavy (10-20)</td><td>Full defense layout + mortars</td></tr>

</tbody>
</table>

<h2>Spending Wealth Wisely</h2>
<p><strong>Hoarded silver invites raids.</strong> Convert wealth into: better weapons and armor, turrets and defenses, medicine, and quality-of-life buildings. A <strong>masterwork weapon for your best shooter</strong> is worth more than 2,000 silver in a stockpile.</p>

<h2>Wealth FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Should I avoid getting rich?</h3>
  <p>No — just spend. Wealth spent on defenses and gear scales your combat power faster than raids scale.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Do colonists count as wealth?</h3>
  <p>Yes — each colonist adds wealth, but they also add firepower. Trained pawns are usually worth the raid increase.</p>
</div>
