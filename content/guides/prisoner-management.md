<p>Good <strong>RimWorld prisoner management</strong> turns captured raiders into colonists. Here's the full workflow for the current <strong>1.6 / Odyssey</strong> version.</p>

<h2>Prisoner Setup</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Cells:</strong> 1 bed + 1 door minimum; a comfortable cell boosts recruitment speed.</li>
<li><strong>Medical:</strong> patch wounds first — healthy prisoners recruit faster.</li>
<li><strong>Food:</strong> deliver meals manually or via a prisoner stockpile.</li>
<li><strong>Security:</strong> doors on prisoners, keep weapons out of reach.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Recruitment Workflow</h2>
<table>
<thead><tr><th>Step</th><th>What to do</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>1. Capture</td><td>Down raiders, rescue and imprison the useful ones</td></tr>
<tr><td>2. Treat</td><td>Patch injuries, feed regularly, keep warm</td></tr>
<tr><td>3. Negotiate</td><td>Assign your highest-Social pawn to recruit</td></tr>
<tr><td>4. Convert (if needed)</td><td>Ideology conversion aligns beliefs before recruiting</td></tr>
<tr><td>5. Release or sell</td><td>Unwanted prisoners can be sold or released for goodwill</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<h2>Prisoner FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Why won't my prisoner recruit?</h3>
  <p>Check that a negotiator is assigned, resistance is dropping, and the prisoner is fed and healthy.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Should I sell prisoners?</h3>
  <p>Yes — slaver traders pay well for healthy prisoners, or release them for faction goodwill.</p>
</div>
