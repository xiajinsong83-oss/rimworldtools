<p><strong>How to recruit prisoners</strong> is one of the most common RimWorld questions. Here's the reliable method for the current <strong>1.6 / Odyssey</strong> version.</p>

<h2>Quick Answer</h2>
<p>Capture an enemy pawn, keep them fed and healthy, reduce their resistance to zero, then recruit them repeatedly until they join.</p>

<h2>Step-by-Step</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Capture</strong> — Down an enemy without killing them, then assign them to a prisoner bed (Medical tab).</li>
<li><strong>Keep them fed</strong> — Prisoners need food and medical care or they'll try to escape.</li>
<li><strong>Reduce resistance</strong> — The Warden will chip away resistance over time. Higher Social skill and a better warden speed this up.</li>
<li><strong>Recruit</strong> — Once resistance is 0, order your warden to recruit; keep trying if it fails.</li>
<li><strong>Watch mood</strong> — Unhappy prisoners recruit slower. Keep their cell clean and their needs met.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Why Prisoners Won't Recruit</h2>
<ul>
<li>Resistance still above zero.</li>
<li>No assigned Warden or prisoner bed.</li>
<li>Prisoner is injured or starving.</li>
<li>Low Social skill on the warden.</li>
</ul>

<h2>RimWorld Recruit FAQ</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Can I recruit prisoners faster?</h3>
  <p>Raise the warden's Social skill, keep the prisoner's needs full, and consider traits that improve mood.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
  <h3>Do prisoners escape?</h3>
  <p>Yes — prisoners may break out when mood drops low. Good food, care and a secure cell help.</p>
</div>
