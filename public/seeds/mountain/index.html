Best Mountain & Defensive Seeds in RimWorld (1.6 & Odyssey)

Mountain seeds are great for defense-minded players. Build your base inside or against a mountain for natural choke points against raids. Use these with the current 1.6 / Odyssey version.

Top Mountain Seeds

SeedMap typeNotes
bedrock30% coverageStrong mountain backing, single entrance defense.
fortress30% coverageDeep mountain, huge interior building space.
gatekeeper20% coverageTight valley, easy to wall off.

Why a Mountain Base Wins

Mountain bases need fewer walls, absorb raids at choke points, and protect against orbital events. The tradeoff is slower expansion and mining work.

Mountain Seed FAQ

Are mountain maps safer?

Yes for raids — natural rock blocks most approaches. But mining takes time and infestations can spawn in deep mountains.
