Designing Your Ideoligion

Precepts shape your colony's mood rules. Pick a consistent theme: a warrior cult pairs raiding-approved with voluntary pain; a high-life colony pairs intense drugs with luxury. Rituals on short cooldowns (drum parties) give steady mood without big costs.

RimWorld Ideology FAQ

Do precepts affect pawn happiness?

Yes — matching precepts give mood bonuses (e.g., eating meat when meat is venerated), while violating them causes penalties.

Which ritual is best early game?

Drum parties are cheap and cooldown-friendly; sacrifice and blinding pay more but need resources or a willing pawn.
