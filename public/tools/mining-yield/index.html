Mining Tips

Your highest-Mining pawn should mine valuable ores (plasteel, gold, components) — skill boosts per-tile yield. Early game, mine steel veins first; components are rarer and worth more per tile.

RimWorld Mining FAQ

Why do my miners get less ore than expected?

Low Mining skill reduces yield per tile. Level up a dedicated miner for best returns.

Are deep drills worth it?

Yes for late-game steel and components, but they need power and a skilled operator.
