How Raids Scale

Raid size scales mostly with colony wealth and colonist count. Too much unused item wealth (clothes, resources, loot) inflates raids beyond your defenses. Spend or consume it to keep raids manageable.

RimWorld Raid FAQ

Why are my raids so big?

High colony wealth is the main driver. Banked silver and stockpiled items all count toward threat.

How do I lower raid threat?

Spend wealth on defenses and consumables, keep stockpiles lean, and avoid hoarding low-value items.
