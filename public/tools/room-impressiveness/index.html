Formula from RimWorld wiki: score = ((avg of 4 factors + lowest factor) / 2) × 100. Higher cleanliness and beauty boost the score fastest.

What Affects Impressiveness

Four factors matter: wealth (silver value), beauty (decorations/quality), space (walkable tiles), and cleanliness. The formula averages all four, then pulls the score down toward the weakest factor — so a dirty room hurts even a rich one.

RimWorld Room Impressiveness FAQ

How do I reach "Wondrously Impressive" (240+)?

You need high values in all four factors. Best value: high-quality sculptures (beauty + wealth) plus a clean, spacious room.

Does cleanliness matter more than beauty?

Cleanliness is a full factor. Keeping a room clean is usually the cheapest way to lift impressiveness from Dull to Normal+.
