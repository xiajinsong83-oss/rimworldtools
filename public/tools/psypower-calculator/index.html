Keeping Psyfocus High

Meditate near your best focus source (anima tree and artefacts beat plain nature) and schedule regular meditation. Strong psycasts burn focus, so bank a surplus before raids — a Master-level psycast can turn a battle.

RimWorld Psyfocus FAQ

What uses psyfocus?

Strong psycasts (berserk pulse, skip, invisibility) consume focus — keep it above the cast threshold when you expect combat.

How do I raise my psycast level?

Anima tree linking, royal favor, and psycast artifacts raise level — higher levels need more focus to fill.
