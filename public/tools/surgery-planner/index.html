How to Make Surgery Safer

Use a clean medical bed in a lit room, a high-Medical doctor, and anesthesia. Failed surgeries damage the patient — never operate with a low-skill doctor or a dirty bed.

RimWorld Surgery FAQ

What happens if surgery fails?

Failed operations damage body parts or kill the patient on catastrophic failure. High skill and cleanliness prevent this.

Which surgeries are worth the risk?

Peg legs, bionic eyes and arms are low-risk, high-value. Heart and brain implants are riskier — wait for a good doctor.
