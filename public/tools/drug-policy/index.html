Safe Drug Rules

Penoxycyline is completely safe and prevents diseases — use it every 5 days. For work buffs, psychite tea is the mildest option. Avoid flake and luciferium unless you have a plan, and never schedule yayo daily.

RimWorld Drug FAQ

What happens if a pawn gets addicted?

They need the drug regularly or suffer withdrawal penalties. Luciferium withdrawal is fatal — keep supply forever.

Can I force a pawn to take drugs?

Yes — set an "assign drug" policy for a specific schedule, or restrict storage so only the pawn can access it.
