How to Avoid Brownouts

Keep generation above consumption by a comfortable margin. Solar and wind fluctuate, so a battery bank and a spare generator prevent sudden brownouts during weather or raids.

RimWorld Power FAQ

Why does my base lose power at night?

Solar panels stop at night. Add batteries or a wind/chemfuel backup to cover the gap.

What's the most power-hungry thing?

Smelters, sun lamps and coolers draw the most. Cut them on a priority schedule if power is tight.
