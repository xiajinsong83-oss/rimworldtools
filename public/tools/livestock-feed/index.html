What to Raise

Chickens are cheap and give eggs; cows give milk for fine meals; muffalo double as pack animals. Avoid huge animals (elephants) until you have a strong food surplus.

RimWorld Livestock FAQ

How much hay does an animal eat per day?

Most animals eat 1-2 nutrition/day of hay; large ones (muffalo, elephant) eat 6-12. Plan fields for winter.

Can animals graze instead of eating hay?

Yes — animals eat grass when allowed to roam. Keep them inside or penned with a hay stockpile for winter.
