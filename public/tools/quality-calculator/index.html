Why Quality Matters

Higher quality items give better stats — a Masterwork weapon deals more damage and an Excellent armor protects better. Inspired creativity is the single biggest boost to legendary chances.

RimWorld Quality FAQ

How do I get Legendary items?

High crafting skill (15+) plus an inspired creativity inspiration gives the best odds. Craft multiple and keep the best.

Does passion affect quality?

Passions boost learning speed more than final quality; skill level is the main driver of quality.
