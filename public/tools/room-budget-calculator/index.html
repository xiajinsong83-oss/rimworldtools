Budget Tips for Impressive Rooms

Plants and sculptures give the best beauty per resource. A clean, spacious room with one good sculpture beats a cluttered one with many cheap items. Your artist's quality tier matters most — a masterwork small sculpture outshines a stack of normal ones.

RimWorld Room FAQ

How much beauty do I need for an impressive bedroom?

Around 100+ total beauty with good space and cleanliness usually crosses into impressive territory.

What's the cheapest way to boost a room?

Plant pots and wall lamps are cheap; a decent small sculpture is the best mid-cost boost.
