Use your highest-Social pawn as negotiator, keep prisoners fed, healthy and warm, and treat them well. Resistance drops faster when prisoners are happy — a comfortable cell beats a dungeon for conversion speed.

RimWorld Recruitment FAQ

Why is my prisoner not being recruited?

No one with Social skill is set to negotiate, or the prisoner's resistance is very high. Assign a negotiator and improve cell conditions.

Can I recruit mechanoids or animals?

No — recruitment works on humanlikes. Animals are tamed, and mechanoids need other systems.
