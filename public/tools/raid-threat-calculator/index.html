Managing Raid Size

Raid size scales mainly with colony wealth. Keep valuable items in stockpiles behind defenses, avoid hoarding thousands of silver, and build kill-boxes, turrets and walls before the 200k-wealth mark when raids get serious.

RimWorld Raid FAQ

What determines raid size?

Wealth, colonist count, days survived and difficulty. More wealth = bigger raids — spend silver on defenses and equipment.

How do I survive a siege?

Mortars counter sieges; keep a trained artillery pawn and shell stockpile ready.
