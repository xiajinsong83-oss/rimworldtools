Why Cooler Count Matters

Too few coolers means food rots in summer; too many wastes power. Wall insulation, double doors and a small airlock let each cooler do more work, so you need fewer.

RimWorld Freezer FAQ

How many coolers for a 200-tile freezer?

At 0°C in a temperate biome, roughly 2 coolers with a safety spare. Hot biomes need more.

Should I set coolers to -10°C?

Only for meat-heavy stockpiles. 0°C preserves most food; -10°C needs more coolers and power.
