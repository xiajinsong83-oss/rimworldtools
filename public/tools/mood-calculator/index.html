Stack the cheap wins: a decent bedroom, good meals, recreation and light. Remove the big drains: pain, darkness, isolation and cold. A stable 50+ mood keeps colonists productive and break-free.

RimWorld Mood FAQ

At what mood do colonists break?

Below about 30, pawns risk mental breaks — the lower the mood, the more severe the break type.

What's the fastest way to fix bad mood?

Give a lavish meal (+8), treat pain, and fix the room — three changes often lift mood above the break line.
