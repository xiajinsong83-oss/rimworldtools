Caravan Tips

Bring a pack animal per 100 kg of cargo and survival meals for the trip. Travel light: heavy caravans crawl and get raided more. Horses and muffalo are the best all-round pack animals.

RimWorld Caravan FAQ

How much can a colonist carry?

About 75 kg baseline; pack animals add 60–250 kg each. Donkeys are cheap early-game carriers.

Why is my caravan so slow?

Heavy load slows you down. Drop low-value cargo or add pack animals.
