How to Research Faster

Stack bench bonuses (advanced bench + multi-analyzer), assign a Too Smart / Fast Learner pawn, and keep the lab lit and clean. Each researcher doubles throughput, so two pawns finish roughly twice as fast.

RimWorld Research FAQ

What should I research first?

Electricity, then Batteries and Solar Panels. Microelectronics opens the mid-game tech tree.

Does the multi-analyzer help?

Yes — it adds a flat research-speed bonus on top of the advanced bench, one of the best returns on investment.
