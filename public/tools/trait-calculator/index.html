    Best Traits for Starting Colonists

    For a strong colony start, favor Industrious, Sanguine and Too Smart. Avoid Pyromaniac, Chemical Interest and Lazy early on — they hurt colony stability.

    RimWorld Trait FAQ

    How many traits can a colonist have?

    Colonists typically have 1–2 traits; some backgrounds or genes add more. Starting pawns have random traits unless modified by mods or prepare-carefully tools.

    Are conflicting traits possible?

    Yes. Some traits are mutually exclusive (e.g. Industrious vs Lazy won't both apply). This calculator helps you pick a coherent build.
