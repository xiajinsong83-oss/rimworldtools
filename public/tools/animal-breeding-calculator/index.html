Breeding Strategy

Chickens and rabbits explode in numbers quickly (watch feed!); pigs are the fast meat farm; cows breed slowly but pay off with milk and beef. Keep one male per several females for steady output.

RimWorld Breeding FAQ

How many animals can I keep?

As many as your feed supply and pen space allow — a breeding herd needs food year-round, including winter hay.

Which animal breeds fastest?

Chickens and rabbits have the shortest cycles; muffalo and elephants take months per birth.
