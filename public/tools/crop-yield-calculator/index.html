Feeding a Colony: What to Plant

Early game: rice fills the freezer fast. Mid game: corn gives the best food per tile on rich soil, potatoes thrive on poor soil, and haygrass feeds animals. Add cotton for clothing and healroot for medicine.

RimWorld Crop FAQ

Which crop gives the most food per tile?

Corn yields the most per tile over a season on fertile soil; rice is fastest but feeds less per tile.

How many tiles of crops does a colonist need?

Roughly 8-12 tiles of rice or corn per colonist, plus buffer for winter and blights.
