Feeding the Colony

Keep a 10-day buffer of meals minimum; more before winter and sieges. Fine meals give a mood boost for little extra cost — upgrade from simple meals as soon as you have a cook and ingredients. Nutrient paste is a great emergency option (no food poisoning).

RimWorld Food FAQ

How much food does a colonist eat per day?

About 1.6-1.8 nutrition — roughly two simple meals. Children and larger pawns vary.

Should I make lavish meals?

Worth it in mid game for the +12 mood, but keep a simple-meal backup for lean seasons.
