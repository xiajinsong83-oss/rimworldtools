Why Armor Type Matters

RimWorld damage splits into sharp (bullets, swords), blunt (maces, rams) and heat (fire, plasma). Armor protects differently against each — marine armor stops sharp best, while flak covers blunt well.

RimWorld Armor FAQ

What's the best armor in the game?

Cataphract and prestige marine armor give the highest protection. For early game, flak vest + helmet is the best value.

Does armor block everything?

No — it reduces damage by a percentage. High armor penetration weapons (charge, rockets) pierce more.
