RimWorld Traits to Avoid — Worst Pawn Traits (1.6 & Odyssey)

Some RimWorld traits actively hurt your colony. Here's the list of traits to avoid, especially on starting colonists, for the current 1.6 / Odyssey version.

Traits to Avoid on Starting Colonists

TraitWhy to avoid
PyromaniacRandomly starts fires — threatens base and pawns.
Chemical InterestRandom drug binges and addiction risk.
LazyGlobal work speed -20%, drags productivity.
WimpDowned easily by pain — a liability in combat.
Greedy / JealousRequire impressive bedrooms or they get unhappy.
AbrasiveFrequent social fights, lowers colony mood.
Too SlowMove speed -20%, slower hauling and defense.

How to Handle Bad Traits

If a good pawn has a bad trait, you can: give them a better bedroom (Greedy/Jealous), keep them away from the stockpile (Pyromaniac), or assign them non-combat work (Wimp). For starts, simply re-roll.

RimWorld Traits FAQ

Can bad traits be removed?

Some MODs and Biotech genes can modify traits, but vanilla pawns keep their traits. Manage around them or pick different colonists.
