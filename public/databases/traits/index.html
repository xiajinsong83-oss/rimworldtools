RimWorld Traits FAQ

Can colonists have more than 2 traits?

Normally 1–2. Some backgrounds, genes (Biotech) or MODs add traits. Starting pawns roll random traits.

Which traits pair well together?

Sanguine + Industrious is a strong all-rounder. Tough + Brawler makes a great frontline fighter.
