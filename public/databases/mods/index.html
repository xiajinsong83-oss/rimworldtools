RimWorld MODs Guide — Essential & QoL MODs (1.6 & Odyssey)

RimWorld has one of the biggest MOD scenes in gaming — over 58,000 MODs on the Steam Workshop. This guide highlights the essential and quality-of-life MODs for the current 1.6 / Odyssey version.

QoL MODs Every Colony Needs

  • Allow Tool — mass commands for hauling and forbidding.
  • Designator Shapes — easier room and wall design.
  • Better Workbench Management — smarter bill automation.
  • Numbers — at-a-glance colony statistics.

MOD Load Order Tips

Keep core frameworks early, content packs after, and UI MODs last. If a MOD breaks a save, disable the newest one first. Back up saves before major MOD changes.

RimWorld MODs FAQ

Are MODs safe with 1.6 / Odyssey?

Most big MODs are updated for 1.6, but always check the Workshop page for version notes and read recent comments.

How do I install MODs?

Subscribe on the Steam Workshop and enable them in the in-game MOD menu. Order matters for some combinations.
