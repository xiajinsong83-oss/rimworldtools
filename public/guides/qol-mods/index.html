RimWorld QoL MODs Every Colony Needs (1.6 & Odyssey)

Quality-of-life (QoL) MODs make RimWorld easier to manage without changing the core game. Here are the best QoL MODs for the current 1.6 / Odyssey version.

Why QoL MODs Matter

RimWorld's default UI is minimal. QoL MODs cut the micromanagement time dramatically, letting you focus on the colony story instead of clicking every haul order.
