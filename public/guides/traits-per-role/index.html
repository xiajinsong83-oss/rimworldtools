Best Traits for Each Role in RimWorld (1.6 & Odyssey)

Match RimWorld traits to roles to get the most from each colonist. Here are the best traits for every key job in the current 1.6 / Odyssey version.

General Principle

Sanguine and Industrious fit almost every role. Add role-specific traits (Tough for fighters, Too Smart for researchers) to specialize.

Role Traits FAQ

Should every colonist be specialized?

No — a few generalists keep the colony flexible. Specialize your best pawns and keep backups.
