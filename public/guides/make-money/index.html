How to Make Money in RimWorld (1.6 & Odyssey)

Wondering how to make money in RimWorld? Here are the most reliable income sources for the current 1.6 / Odyssey version.

Quick Answer

Tailoring and crafting (clothes, weapons, art) are the best stable income. Grow cash crops like smokeleaf or cotton, then sell goods at a trade outpost or caravan.

Best Income Sources

MethodIncome potentialEffort
Tailoring (craft clothes)High, steadyLow–medium
Art / sculpturesVery highMedium (needs Artistic pawn)
Cash crops (smokeleaf, cotton)HighLow
Drugs (yayo, flake)Very highLow (has risks)
Caravan tradingHighMedium–high

Tips

  • Sell to outlanders and settlements — prices are better than at your own colony.
  • Keep stockpiles lean: excess wealth inflates raids (see our raid planner).
  • Invest profits in defenses and better gear, not hoarding.

RimWorld Economy FAQ

Is selling drugs worth it?

Yes — high profit, but keep your own colonists away from them to avoid addiction.
