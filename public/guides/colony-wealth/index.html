RimWorld Colony Wealth Guide — Manage Wealth, Reduce Raids (1.6 & Odyssey)

RimWorld colony wealth is the single biggest driver of raid size. Learn what counts, how it scales, and how to spend it — for the current 1.6 / Odyssey version.

What Counts as Wealth

  • Buildings and items: everything constructed and every item stored.
  • Colonists and animals: pawns add significant wealth.
  • Silver and valuables: raw silver, gold, components count fully.

How Wealth Scales Raids

Wealth tierRaid trendAdvice
Under 50kLight raids (1-3 raiders)Focus on food and weapons
50k-200kModerate (4-8)Build walls and turrets
200k-500kHeavy (10-20)Full defense layout + mortars
500k+Massive / siegesSpend wealth on defenses & pawns

Spending Wealth Wisely

Hoarded silver invites raids. Convert wealth into: better weapons and armor, turrets and defenses, medicine, and quality-of-life buildings. A masterwork weapon for your best shooter is worth more than 2,000 silver in a stockpile.

Wealth FAQ

Should I avoid getting rich?

No — just spend. Wealth spent on defenses and gear scales your combat power faster than raids scale.

Do colonists count as wealth?

Yes — each colonist adds wealth, but they also add firepower. Trained pawns are usually worth the raid increase.
