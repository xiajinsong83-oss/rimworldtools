RimWorld Prisoner Management Guide — Recruit, Convert & Keep (1.6 & Odyssey)

Good RimWorld prisoner management turns captured raiders into colonists. Here's the full workflow for the current 1.6 / Odyssey version.

Prisoner Setup

  • Cells: 1 bed + 1 door minimum; a comfortable cell boosts recruitment speed.
  • Medical: patch wounds first — healthy prisoners recruit faster.
  • Food: deliver meals manually or via a prisoner stockpile.
  • Security: doors on prisoners, keep weapons out of reach.

Recruitment Workflow

StepWhat to do
1. CaptureDown raiders, rescue and imprison the useful ones
2. TreatPatch injuries, feed regularly, keep warm
3. NegotiateAssign your highest-Social pawn to recruit
4. Convert (if needed)Ideology conversion aligns beliefs before recruiting
5. Release or sellUnwanted prisoners can be sold or released for goodwill

Prisoner FAQ

Why won't my prisoner recruit?

Check that a negotiator is assigned, resistance is dropping, and the prisoner is fed and healthy.

Should I sell prisoners?

Yes — slaver traders pay well for healthy prisoners, or release them for faction goodwill.
