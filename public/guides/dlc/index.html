Which RimWorld DLC Should You Buy First?

There are 5 RimWorld DLCs (including Odyssey). If you're wondering which to buy first, this guide ranks them by value for different playstyles.

All 5 RimWorld DLCs

DLCAddsBest for
RoyaltyEmpire faction, titles, psionics, mech clustersPlayers who like structured goals and magic-like powers.
IdeologyBelief systems, rituals, social roles, dryadsStory and roleplay players who enjoy customizing colonies.
BiotechChildren, mechanoids, genetics, xenohumansLong-term colony builders who love depth and family lines.
AnomalyHorror entities, containment, alternate endingsPlayers who want danger, mystery and a darker tone.
OdysseyGravships, inter-planet travel, moving colonies, space stationsExplorers who want to leave the planet and travel.

Our Recommendation

Biotech offers the most depth and replay value for most players. If you love exploration, Odyssey adds a whole new endgame. For a lighter, cheaper first step, Royalty is a safe pick.

DLC FAQ

Can I play with multiple DLCs together?

Yes — all DLCs are designed to work together and with vanilla saves.

Do DLCs affect performance?

Each adds content; combining all can increase load slightly, but RimWorld remains lightweight.
