How to Recruit Prisoners in RimWorld (1.6 & Odyssey)

How to recruit prisoners is one of the most common RimWorld questions. Here's the reliable method for the current 1.6 / Odyssey version.

Quick Answer

Capture an enemy pawn, keep them fed and healthy, reduce their resistance to zero, then recruit them repeatedly until they join.

Step-by-Step

  1. Capture — Down an enemy without killing them, then assign them to a prisoner bed (Medical tab).
  2. Keep them fed — Prisoners need food and medical care or they'll try to escape.
  3. Reduce resistance — The Warden will chip away resistance over time. Higher Social skill and a better warden speed this up.
  4. Recruit — Once resistance is 0, order your warden to recruit; keep trying if it fails.
  5. Watch mood — Unhappy prisoners recruit slower. Keep their cell clean and their needs met.

Why Prisoners Won't Recruit

  • Resistance still above zero.
  • No assigned Warden or prisoner bed.
  • Prisoner is injured or starving.
  • Low Social skill on the warden.

RimWorld Recruit FAQ

Can I recruit prisoners faster?

Raise the warden's Social skill, keep the prisoner's needs full, and consider traits that improve mood.

Do prisoners escape?

Yes — prisoners may break out when mood drops low. Good food, care and a secure cell help.
